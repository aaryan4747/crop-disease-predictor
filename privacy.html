Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 08, 2026

Welcome to Crop Care AI (accessible at crop-disease-predictor-mu.vercel.app). We respect the privacy of our agricultural community and platform users. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal and non-personal information collected and recorded by Crop Care AI and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better agricultural diagnostic tools, localized weather advisories, and Mandi price updates to farmers:

  • Uploaded Images: Plant leaf photos uploaded for disease diagnosis are processed locally or via serverless diagnostic endpoints. We do not sell or share your private crop photos.
  • Location Data (GPS): If enabled by the user, location coordinates are used solely to fetch local weather forecasts, rain probability, and nearby agricultural store locations.
  • Usage & Device Information: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and pages visited via standard web analytics.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

Crop Care AI uses Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site or other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Crop Care AI, sent directly to your browser.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Crop Care AI follows a standard procedure of using log files and privacy-focused analytics. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All such data is non-personally identifiable.

4. Children's Information

Crop Care AI does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information, we encourage you to contact us immediately.

5. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us via email at support@cropcareai.com or visit our Contact Us page.
