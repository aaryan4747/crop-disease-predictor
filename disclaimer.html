Agronomic & Medical Safety Disclaimer

Last updated: August 08, 2026

The information provided by Crop Care AI on crop-disease-predictor-mu.vercel.app is for general educational and agricultural advisory purposes only.

1. AI Pathology Predictions

Automated computer vision diagnostic confidence scores are calculated through algorithmic feature matching against ICAR and FAO plant pathology literature. While our models are tested for high validation benchmarks, environmental factors (such as camera lighting, leaf dust, or secondary infections) can affect image analysis. Predictions should be treated as diagnostic aids rather than absolute laboratory diagnoses.

2. Chemical Dosage & Pesticide Safety

All chemical fungicide, insecticide, and bactericide spray guidelines listed on Crop Care AI adhere to CIBRC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee) standards. However:

  • Farmers must strictly observe pre-harvest interval (PHI) waiting days before picking produce.
  • Always wear protective gloves, masks, and eye gear when mixing active ingredients.
  • Confirm chemical dilutions with your local Agricultural Extension Officer (AEO) or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) officer before spraying high-concentration solutions.

3. Mandi Market Prices

Mandi price rates displayed on Crop Care AI are benchmark estimates aggregated from regional APMC feeds and AGMARKNET portal feeds. Actual market transaction prices vary depending on daily auctions, crop moisture content, and crop grade quality.
