About Crop Care AI

Crop Care AI is a free, digital agronomy intelligence and plant pathology diagnostic platform built specifically to support farmers, agricultural researchers, and extension officers across India.

🌾 Our Mission

Our goal is to democratize high-precision agricultural pathology intelligence. By providing instant leaf photo diagnosis, localized weather risk forecasts, 16-state Mandi price benchmark tracking, and certified remedy guidelines, we empower farmers to protect crop yields and reduce chemical wastage.

🔬 Standardized Scientific Research

All disease symptom definitions, organic remedies, and chemical spray protocols on Crop Care AI are standardized against authoritative plant pathology literature published by:

  • ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)
  • FAO (Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations)
  • ANGRAU & TNAU State Agricultural University Research Bulletins
  • CIBRC Approved Fungicide & Bactericide Labeling Guidelines

🌐 Multi-Lingual Regional Support

To ensure accessibility for all Indian farming communities, Crop Care AI supports 11 major regional languages including English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Punjabi, and Odia.
