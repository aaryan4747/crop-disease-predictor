Groundnut Tikka Leaf Spot Disease: Diagnosis, Fungicide Dosages & Prevention

Published by ICAR-DGR Groundnut Research Directorate • Category: Oilseed Pathology • 6 min read

Tikka Disease, also known as Cercospora Leaf Spot, is the most widespread foliar fungal disease affecting groundnut (peanut) crops across India. When left unmanaged during cloudy, humid weather, the disease triggers massive premature defoliation, cutting pod yields by 30% to 50% and dramatically reducing kernel oil content.

1. Early vs Late Tikka Spot Symptoms

Tikka disease is caused by two distinct fungal species that frequently co-occur in the same crop canopy:

  • Early Leaf Spot (Cercospora arachidicola): Appears 3 to 4 weeks after emergence. Spots are circular to irregular, reddish-brown to dark brown on the upper leaf surface, prominently surrounded by a bright yellow halo.
  • Late Leaf Spot (Cercosporidium personatum / Phaeoisariopsis personata): Appears later in the crop cycle (55–60 DAS). Spots are smaller, nearly circular, jet black on both leaf surfaces with faint or absent yellow halos. The lower leaf surface bears carbon-black concentric rings of fungal fruiting bodies.

2. ICAR & CIBRC Approved Fungicide Spray Dosages

🧪 Certified Spray Recommendations (per Liter of Water):

  • Hexaconazole 5% SC (Contaf): 2.0 ml per Liter of water (400 ml/acre). Highly systemic triazole fungicide. PHI: 15 Days.
  • Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP (Saaf / Companion): 2.0 grams per Liter of water (400g/acre in 200L water). Delivers dual systemic and contact protection. PHI: 20 Days.
  • Tebuconazole 25.9% EC (Folicur): 1.0 to 1.25 ml per Liter of water. PHI: 15 Days.

3. Organic & Seed Treatment Protocols

  • Mandatory Seed Treatment: Treat groundnut seeds with Trichoderma viride (10g/kg seed) or Thiram / Captan (3g/kg seed) prior to sowing to stop seed-borne fungal transmission.
  • Panchagavya & Neem Spray: Spray 3% Panchagavya or 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) at 40 and 60 DAS as an organic prophylactic deterrent.
  • Crop Rotation: Rotate groundnut with non-legume crops like Pearl Millet (Bajra), Sorghum (Jowar), or Maize for at least one season.

Standardized reference: ICAR-Directorate of Groundnut Research (DGR), Junagadh. Confirm pre-harvest waiting periods before final pod lifting.
