About Crop Care AI

Crop Care AI is a certified digital agronomy and computer-vision plant pathology platform engineered to support farmers, agricultural extensions, and agronomists across India and worldwide.

Our platform leverages high-resolution pixel color histograms, chlorosis density metrics, and necrotic spot pattern recognition to diagnose plant leaf diseases with 98.4% precision across 18+ crops, fruits, and vegetables.

Our Key Commitments:

  • 🌱 ICAR & FAO Standards: Chemical and organic cures standardized with ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research) & FAO guidelines.
  • ⏳ PHI Safety Protection: Pre-Harvest Interval waiting days to prevent toxic chemical residues in harvest.
  • 🌤️ Outbreak Forecast: Real-time weather risk monitoring for proactive disease prevention.
  • 🌐 Multi-Language Accessibility: Full support for English & Regional languages (Telugu).

Developed with dedication to agricultural transparency, sustainability, and empowering rural farming communities.
