🌾 Most Grown Crops, Common Diseases & Side-by-Side Remedies

Comprehensive guide for major regional crops showing key diseases alongside organic and chemical medicine spray solutions.

🌾

Rice / Paddy (వరి)

Rice Blast (వరి అగ్గి తెగులు)

🔍 Symptoms

Spindle-shaped diamond spots with ash-gray center on leaves; breaking stem nodes.

🌿 Organic Remedy

Pseudomonas fluorescens (10g/L water) foliar spray.

🧪 Chemical Medicine

Tricyclazole 75% WP (Baan / Beam) @ 0.6g/L water.

🌶️

Chilli (మిరప)

Chilli Leaf Curl & Dieback (ఆకు ముడుత)

🔍 Symptoms

Upward boat-shaped curling, stunted growth, and black dying branch tips.

🌿 Organic Remedy

5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 50ml/L) + 1ml soap.

🧪 Chemical Medicine

Fipronil 5% SC (1.5ml/L) or Amistar Top (1ml/L).

🍉

Watermelon (పుచ్చకాయ)

Anthracnose & Wilt (పుచ్చకాయ నల్లమచ్చ)

🔍 Symptoms

Dark brown circular spots on leaves and sunken rind spots on watermelon fruit.

🌿 Organic Remedy

Trichoderma viride root drench + Copper Hydroxide (2.5g/L).

🧪 Chemical Medicine

Azoxystrobin 23% SC (Amistar) @ 1ml/L water.

☁️

Cotton (ప్రత్తి)

Alternaria Leaf Spot (ఆకు మచ్చ తెగులు)

🔍 Symptoms

Brown circular spots with red borders on leaves leading to shot-hole leaf drops.

🌿 Organic Remedy

Fermented Cow Urine + Buttermilk spray (50ml/L water).

🧪 Chemical Medicine

Copper Oxychloride (Blitox 3g/L) or Propiconazole (1ml/L).

🥜

Groundnut (వేరుశనగ)

Tikka Leaf Spot (తిక్కా ఆకు మచ్చ)

🔍 Symptoms

Dark circular leaf spots with yellow halo causing severe leaf shedding.

🌿 Organic Remedy

Trichoderma viride + Vermicompost soil application.

🧪 Chemical Medicine

SAAF (2g/L) or Nativo (Tebuconazole + Trifloxystrobin @ 0.75g/L).

🍌

Banana (అరటి)

Sigatoka Leaf Spot (సిగటోకా తెగులు)

🔍 Symptoms

Yellow-green streaks parallel to leaf veins turning dark brown & dry.

🌿 Organic Remedy

Horticultural Mineral Oil spray (10ml/L) + De-trashing.

🧪 Chemical Medicine

Tilt (Propiconazole 1ml/L + 10ml Mineral oil).
