Best Fungicides & Spray Dosages for Tomato Early Blight & Late Blight

Compiled by the Crop Care AI editorial team from public ICAR and state agricultural university advisories • Category: Vegetable Pathology • 6 min read

Tomato cultivation across major growing hubs like Kolar (Karnataka) and Madanapalle (Andhra Pradesh) suffers recurring damage from two major fungal diseases: Early Blight (Alternaria solani) and Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans). Timely diagnostic detection and correct fungicide selection are crucial for preserving fruit quality and preventing yield loss.

1. Diagnostic Differences

  • Early Blight (Alternaria solani): Concentric target ring spots (bullseye pattern) on lower older leaves with yellow halos.
  • Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans): Irregular dark water-soaked lesions expanding rapidly during cool rainy weather with white mold growth on leaf undersides.

2. Commonly Recommended Fungicide Spray Dosages

🧪 Commonly Used Chemical Spray Options (per Liter of Water):

  • Bayer Nativo (Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG): 0.75 grams per Liter of water. PHI: 7 Days.
  • Mancozeb 75% WP (Indofil M-45): 2.5 grams per Liter of water. PHI: 14 Days.
  • Copper Oxychloride 50% WP (Blitox): 3.0 grams per Liter of water. PHI: 10 Days.

3. Cultural & Organic Management

  • Drip irrigation to minimize free water on foliage.
  • Remove infected lower leaves and burn or bury them away from the main plot.

Disclaimer: Always consult your local Agricultural Extension Officer (AEO) or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) scientist before spraying high-concentration agro-chemicals.
