Onion Purple Blotch (Alternaria porri): Symptoms, Prevention and Spray Plan

Compiled by the Crop Care AI editorial team from public ICAR and state agricultural university advisories • Category: Vegetable Pathology • 6 min read

Purple Blotch, caused by the fungus Alternaria porri, is a common leaf disease of onion and garlic in warm, humid weather. It reduces the green leaf area, so bulbs stay small. Severe infection can also cause the leaf to break and the bulbs to store poorly.

1. Symptoms

  • Small white sunken spots: the first lesions are small, water-soaked, whitish spots on leaves and flower stalks.
  • Purple centre with yellow border: spots enlarge into elongated lesions with a purple-brown centre surrounded by a yellow zone, often with concentric rings.
  • Leaf drying from the tip: lesions can girdle the leaf, which then collapses above the lesion.
  • Link with thrips: thrips feeding leaves small wounds through which the fungus enters easily, so thrips control reduces blotch.

2. Conditions that favour the disease

Warm weather (about 25 to 30 degrees C) with high humidity, long leaf wetness from dew or overhead irrigation, dense planting, and excess nitrogen all increase the risk. Crop residue from the previous season carries the fungus over.

3. Cultural prevention

  • Crop rotation: avoid onion or garlic in the same field for two to three seasons.
  • Healthy seed and seedlings: treat seed with thiram at about 2 to 3 g per kg before sowing, and use clean nursery beds.
  • Reduce leaf wetness: prefer drip or furrow irrigation, wider spacing and good drainage.
  • Balanced nutrition: avoid excess nitrogen and include potash and sulphur as per soil test.
  • Field sanitation: remove and destroy infected residue after harvest.

4. Commonly used fungicides

Commonly used sprays (per Litre of water)

Add a sticker, as onion leaves are waxy. Begin at first symptoms and repeat at 10 to 15 day intervals, alternating between different fungicide groups.

  • Mancozeb 75% WP: 2.5 g per Litre (protective).
  • Tebuconazole 25.9% EC: 1.0 ml per Litre.
  • Propiconazole 25% EC: 1.0 ml per Litre.
  • Azoxystrobin 23% SC: 1.0 ml per Litre.

Control thrips at the same time, since fewer wounds on the leaf means fewer infections.
