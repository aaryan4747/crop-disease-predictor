Crop Protection Guides for Indian Farmers

Compiled by the Crop Care AI editorial team from public ICAR and state agricultural university advisories • Category: Guide Library • 5 min read

This library collects practical crop protection guides for Indian farmers. Each guide explains how to recognise the problem, how to scout and decide when to act, which control options are commonly used, and how to prevent it next season. They are written for education and are based on public ICAR and state agricultural university advisories.

Use the diagnostic tool on the home page to photograph a leaf and get a first suggestion, then read the matching guide here. For any spray, read the product label and check with your local Agricultural Extension Officer or KVK.
